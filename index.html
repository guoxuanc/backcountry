The crux of the trip. The trail gains 4,462 feet across 10 miles with almost no recovery terrain. The sustained push begins around mile 4 through Tiltil Valley and continues over the ridge to the Jack Main Canyon rim. A long, physical day — plan for a full 8 hours.

Water Conditions · Day 3 Focus
Moderate–Low Risk · 2026

2026 is a low snow year. The Tuolumne drainage finished the May 1 survey at just 25% of normal, and snowmelt ran early this spring. By mid-May the Hetch Hetchy gauge is at its historical median and declining.

Falls Creek runs alongside your Day 3 route for miles 2 through 5 at roughly 7,600 ft. In a normal May this section runs thigh to waist deep. This year expect knee depth at most. Cross before noon when diurnal melt begins to peak.

  • Hip belt unclipped, poles out before any ford
  • Tiltil Valley is already flooded at lower elevation — Jack Main Canyon lags behind
  • No precipitation in the forecast May 16–19
  • From Night 2 ridge camp, listen for Falls Creek below before committing to the descent
