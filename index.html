Water Conditions · Day 3 Focus
Moderate–Low Risk · 2026

2026 is a below-average snow year, with the Tuolumne drainage finishing the May 1 survey at just 25% of normal. Snowmelt ran early this spring, compressing what is usually a longer runoff window into a shorter pulse. As of mid-May, the Hetch Hetchy gauge is sitting right at its historical median for this time of year and trending down. ↗ snoflo

Falls Creek through Jack Main Canyon is where flooding concentrates in bigger snow years. Your Day 3 route runs alongside it for roughly miles 2 through 5, at around 7,600 ft. In a normal May that section can run thigh to waist deep. This year, with snowpack well below average and a warm dry forecast through your trip, knee-depth crossings are the more realistic scenario. ↗ AllTrails

  • Cross Falls Creek before noon — snowmelt peaks in early afternoon
  • Poles out and hip belt unclipped before any ford
  • Tiltil Valley is already flooded at lower elevation; Jack Main Canyon lags behind
  • No precipitation forecast May 16–19; flows will keep declining
  • From Night 2 ridge camp you can listen for creek volume before committing to the descent ↗ HST
Camps
Rancheria Falls
Night 1 · May 16 · 4,501 ft · Zone 86
The campsite area spreads out around the base of the falls. Wander off the main path and you will find more private spots with existing fire rings tucked behind granite outcrops. It fills on weekends so arriving before 3 pm is worth it. ↗ AllTrails
Jack Main Canyon Ridge
Night 2 · May 17 · 8,458 ft · Zone 93
Exposed high-bench camping with open views north into the canyon. The position is tactically useful too — from the ridge you can listen for Falls Creek below and get a sense of water volume before committing to the descent in the morning. ↗ HST forum
Lower Frog Creek
Night 3 · May 18 · 6,283 ft · Zone 88
A still pool along Frog Creek serves as the water source. At 6,283 ft you are below treeline and noticeably warmer than the two previous camps. The payoff is a short 4.9 mile walk out the next morning, mostly downhill through the Beehive switchbacks to the dam. ↗ AllTrails
